Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is arguably the best dish in the world.

It is made with pasta, Bolognese, and béchamel sauce. The recipe below, is the original italian one.

Ingredients (for 8 servings):

    For the pasta:

  • Fresh egg lasagna sheets

    • For the Bolognese sauce:

  • Ground beef: 700 g
  • Tomato passata: 350 g
  • Fresh pork belly: 270 g
  • Carrots: 110 g
  • Celery: 110 g
  • Yellow onions: 110 g
  • Vegetable broth: 350 g
  • Red wine: 110 g
  • Double concentrated tomato paste: 30 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil: 90 ml
  • Fine salt: to taste
  • Black pepper: to taste

    • For the béchamel sauce

  • Whole milk: 800 g
  • Butter: 50 g
  • All-purpose flour: 50 g
  • Nutmeg: to taste
  • Fine salt: to taste

    • For the topping:

  • Parmigiano Reggiano DOP (grated): 150 g
  • Butter: to taste

Steps

  1. Make the ragù: Finely dice the carrot, celery, and onion. In a pot with hot olive oil, brown the minced pork belly (pancetta), then add the chopped vegetables and cook gently until softened.
  2. Add the minced beef, cook until well browned and dry, then pour in the red wine and let it completely evaporate.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, tomato passata, and hot vegetable broth. Cover and simmer on low heat for about 2 hours, seasoning with salt and pepper halfway through.
  4. Make the béchamel sauce: In a small saucepan, heat the milk without boiling. In another pan, melt the butter, stir in the flour all at once, and cook for a few minutes until golden. Gradually whisk in the hot milk, season with salt and nutmeg, and cook until smooth and creamy.
  5. Parboil the pasta: Cook the fresh lasagna sheets in lightly salted boiling water for about 30-40 seconds each, then drain and lay them flat on a clean kitchen towel to remove excess water.
  6. Assemble: Spread a thin layer of béchamel and ragù on the bottom of a 30x20 cm baking dish. Add a pasta sheet, then layer with béchamel, ragù, and grated Parmigiano Reggiano. Repeat to create 6 layers.
  7. Bake: Cover the top layer with the remaining ragù, plenty of Parmigiano Reggiano, and a few small cubes of butter. Bake in a preheated oven at 170°C (340°F) for about 40 minutes, then let it rest for a few minutes before serving.
