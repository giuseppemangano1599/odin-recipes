Vegan Brownies

Description

Are you allergic to eggs or dairies? No problem! This recipe is perfect!

Made with cocoa powder, these easy vegan brownies are rich, fudgy, and packed with deep chocolate flavor.

Ingredients (for 15 servings):

  • All-purpose flour: 250 g
  • Granulated white sugar: 400 g
  • Good-quality unsweetened cocoa powder, sifted: 70 g
  • Baking powder: 5 g
  • Fine salt: 4 g
  • Hot water or coffee: 240 ml
  • Vegetable oil 120 ml
  • Vanilla extract 10 ml

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with baking spray; line bottom with parchment paper.
  2. Whisk flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl until combined.
  3. Pour in water (or coffee), vegetable oil, and vanilla; mix until well-blended. Spread batter evenly in the prepared dish.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until top is no longer shiny and center feels just set when gently pressed, about 30 minutes.
  5. Let cool for at least 10 minutes before cutting into 15 squares. Enjoy!
